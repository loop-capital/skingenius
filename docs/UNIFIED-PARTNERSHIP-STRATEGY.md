## Legal Framework

### What We Can Do (Cash-Pay Cosmetic)

- **Referral-only:** Link to providers, collect marketing fees ✅
- **Directory/listing fees:** Zocdoc model, Sorensen/Sisselman precedent ✅
- **Manufacturer attribution:** CPL/CPA for product recommendations ✅
- **Certification badges:** Manufacturer-backed, verified ✅

### What We Cannot Do

- **Pay physicians per referral:** Violates fee-splitting laws (CA B&P §650) ❌
- **Employ physicians directly:** CPOM violations in most states ❌
- **Handle prescriptions:** Requires affiliated medical entities ❌
- **Make diagnostic claims:** FDA regulatory risk ❌

### Structure That Works

SKINgenius is a **technology platform** that:
1. Provides AI-powered skin analysis (decision support, not diagnosis)
2. Recommends products based on condition-ingredient mapping
3. Connects users to verified providers (derms, plastic surgeons, certified injectors)
4. Tracks attribution for manufacturers
5. Collects deposits and platform fees as merchant of record

Providers are **independent contractors** or ** marketplace participants**, not employees.
