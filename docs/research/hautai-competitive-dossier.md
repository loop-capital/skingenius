## Executive Summary

Haut.AI (HautAI OÜ) is an Estonian generative AI-powered skincare personalization platform that has emerged as the dominant B2B SaaS player in the beauty technology market. Founded in 2018 by scientist Anastasia Georgievskaya and co-founder Konstantin Kiselev, the company has built a comprehensive three-product platform—AI Skin Analysis, Skin.Chat, and SkinGPT—that serves 130+ beauty brands and retailers globally.

With a modest €2 million seed round (November 2023) from strategic investors including LongeVC and Grupo Boticário Ventures, Haut.AI has achieved remarkable traction: partnerships with Neutrogena, Ulta Beauty, Beiersdorf, NAOS (Bioderma), OLAY, Noom, and a landmark rollout across ~4,000 O Boticário stores in Brazil demonstrating an 80% lift in average skincare order value.

The company operates on Microsoft Azure infrastructure, claims 98% diagnostic accuracy validated against expert-labeled datasets, and has trained its models on 3+ million data points across 150+ facial biomarkers. Its patented Skin Atlas anonymization technology addresses growing privacy concerns in facial image analysis.

For SKINgenius, Haut.AI represents a **HIGH-THREAT** competitor in the B2B white-label skin analysis space, with significant advantages in enterprise client acquisition, generative AI capabilities (SkinGPT), and strategic investor relationships that convert into production contracts. However, SKINgenius may differentiate through specialized clinical focus, direct-to-consumer pathways, or specific geographic/vertical markets where Haut.AI's enterprise sales motion is less optimized.

## 2. Products & Technology

### 2.1 Core Product Suite

Haut.AI operates a three-product platform, each addressing different touchpoints in the beauty consumer journey:

#### Product 1: AI Skin Analysis
The flagship product provides clinical-grade skin diagnostics from consumer selfies, converting them into personalized product recommendations.

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Parameters Analyzed:** 150+ skin health and beauty metrics
- **Training Data:** 3+ million data points
- **Claimed Accuracy:** 98% diagnostic accuracy (validated against expert-labeled datasets)
- **Skin Type Coverage:** Effective across diverse skin types and tones (Fitzpatrick I–VI)
- **Deployment Options:** API, web widget, mobile SDK, in-store kiosk
- **Integration:** Available as white-label solution or API for e-commerce platforms, mobile apps, or in-store kiosks

**Key Features:**
- Real-time analysis from selfie upload
- Comprehensive skin health report generation
- Personalized skincare routine recommendations
- Product recommendation engine (maps to client SKUs)
- Before/after tracking capabilities

#### Product 2: Skin.Chat
An always-on AI skincare companion that guides consumers from skin concern to checkout.

**Capabilities:**
- Personalized routines and product advice
- Brand-aligned communication (adapts to each brand's tone and style)
- Recommend, explain, and add-to-cart without leaving the chat
- Natural language interface for skincare questions
- Integration with e-commerce platforms for seamless purchasing

#### Product 3: SkinGPT
Generative AI that visually simulates skincare product results on real skin.

**Functionality:**
- Visual simulation of product effects on user's own skin
- Before/after visualization for marketing and engagement
- Product efficacy demonstration
- Virtual try-on capabilities for skincare products

### 2.2 Technology Stack

**Infrastructure:** Microsoft Azure cloud platform
**AI/ML Framework:** Proprietary deep learning models for skin analysis
**Data Processing:** Patented Skin Atlas anonymization technology
**Security & Compliance:**
- ISO 27001:2022 (Information Security Management)
- ISO 13485 (Medical Devices Quality Management)
- UKCA Class I medical device certification
- GDPR compliance for EU operations

### 2.3 Data & Accuracy Claims

| Metric | Claim | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Training Data Points | 3+ million | Across diverse demographics |

### 2.4 Privacy Technology

**Skin Atlas** — Patented anonymization technology that processes facial images without storing identifiable personal data. This addresses growing consumer privacy concerns and regulatory requirements (GDPR, CCPA) for facial image processing.

## 3. Pricing & Revenue Model

### 3.1 Business Model

Haut.AI operates on a **B2B SaaS model** with usage-based pricing:

| Tier | Target | Pricing Structure |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Indie Beauty Brands** | Small to medium brands | Usage-based, scales with scan volume |
| **Enterprise** | Global beauty retailers | Custom pricing with dedicated account manager |
| **API Access** | Technology integrators | Pay-per-use or volume-based API pricing |

**Key Pricing Characteristics:**
- **Not publicly disclosed:** Exact pricing requires direct inquiry
- **Usage-based:** Scales with AI skincare capabilities growth
- **White-label included:** Custom branded solutions for enterprise clients
- **Dedicated support:** Enterprise clients receive dedicated account managers

### 3.2 Revenue Streams

1. **SaaS Subscriptions:** Monthly/annual platform fees based on usage volume
2. **API Licensing:** Per-call or volume-based API access fees
3. **Professional Services:** Implementation, customization, and integration services
4. **Enterprise Contracts:** Multi-year deals with major beauty brands and retailers

### 3.3 Competitive Pricing Context

Industry benchmarks from comparable AI skin analysis providers:
- **ScanSkinAI:** $59.99/year (Starter) to $899.85/year (Professional, 15 seats, 500 scans/month)
- **Haut.AI:** Likely higher-tier given enterprise focus and strategic investor pricing

| **Clinical Focus** | ❌ General beauty focus | ✅ Medical-grade positioning |
| **Geographic Focus** | ✅ Global (Estonia HQ, US clients) | ✅ US-focused initially |

### 5.2 Haut.AI Advantages

1. **Enterprise Traction:** 130+ brands, including Neutrogena, Ulta, Beiersdorf
2. **Generative AI Leadership:** SkinGPT creates visual simulations SKINgenius cannot match
3. **Strategic Investors:** Investor relationships convert to production contracts
4. **Privacy Technology:** Patented Skin Atlas anonymization addresses regulatory concerns
5. **Technical Accuracy:** 150+ parameters vs. SKINgenius's 25 conditions
6. **Time to Market:** 6+ years of operation and iteration
7. **Compliance:** ISO 13485, UKCA Class I, ISO 27001 certifications

### 5.3 SKINgenius Advantages

1. **Clinical Positioning:** Medical-grade focus differentiates from general beauty
2. **Provider Network:** Certified injector network creates treatment closure
3. **Commerce Integration:** Square-powered deposits, orders, inventory management
4. **Manufacturer Attribution:** CPL/CPA revenue model unique to SKINgenius
5. **B2C Direct:** Consumer app with direct revenue potential
6. **Dermatology Partnership:** Path to clinical credibility and prescription workflows
7. **Lower Cost Structure:** Self-funded, leaner operations

### 5.4 Threat Assessment

**THREAT LEVEL: HIGH**

Haut.AI poses the most significant competitive threat in the following scenarios:

1. **If Haut.AI adds provider matching:** Their existing brand relationships and consumer data would make them immediate competitors
2. **If Haut.AI enters clinical dermatology:** Their accuracy and compliance certifications would challenge SKINgenius's medical-grade positioning
3. **If Haut.AI launches B2C:** Their brand recognition and enterprise relationships could accelerate consumer acquisition
4. **If Haut.AI partners with dermatology networks:** They could replicate SKINgenius's provider network strategy

**THREAT LEVEL: MEDIUM**

In these scenarios, competition is indirect:

1. **B2B white-label market:** Haut.AI owns this segment; SKINgenius is not competing here yet
2. **Enterprise beauty brands:** Haut.AI serves brands SKINgenius does not target directly
3. **International markets:** Haut.AI's Estonia HQ and global clients give geographic advantages

## 6. Strategic Recommendations

### 6.1 Immediate Actions (0–30 Days)

1. **Monitor Haut.AI Product Roadmap:** Track announcements for provider matching, B2C launch, or clinical features
2. **Analyze SkinGPT:** Evaluate feasibility of implementing before/after simulation in SKINgenius
3. **Benchmark Accuracy:** Compare SKINgenius's 25-condition model against Haut.AI's 150+ parameter claims
4. **Review Compliance:** Assess gaps in ISO 13485, ISO 27001 certifications vs. Haut.AI

### 6.2 Short-Term Differentiation (1–3 Months)

1. **Implement Before/After Simulation:** Add visual product result simulation to SKINgenius scan flow
2. **Launch AI Chat Companion:** Build Skin.Chat equivalent for SKINgenius users
3. **Clinical Focus Deepening:** Partner with dermatology societies (AAD) to differentiate from beauty-focused competitors
4. **Provider Network Acceleration:** Fast-track certified injector network to create treatment closure Haut.AI cannot match

### 6.3 Medium-Term Strategy (3–6 Months)

1. **B2B White-Label Pilot:** Test white-label skin analysis for smaller dermatology practices
2. **Privacy Technology:** Implement facial image anonymization comparable to Skin Atlas
3. **Enterprise Sales Motion:** Develop sales materials targeting beauty brands that compete with Haut.AI clients
4. **Geographic Expansion:** Consider European market entry where Haut.AI is strongest

### 6.4 Long-Term Positioning (6–18 Months)

1. **Clinical Trial Validation:** Conduct independent studies validating SKINgenius accuracy against dermatologist diagnoses
2. **Regulatory Clearance:** Pursue FDA clearance or CE marking for clinical diagnostic claims
3. **Strategic Partnerships:** Seek beauty brand partnerships that complement (not compete with) Haut.AI relationships
4. **Acquisition Consideration:** Evaluate if Haut.AI becomes acquisition target if SKINgenius raises significant capital
