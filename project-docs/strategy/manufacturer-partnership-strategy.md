## Executive Summary

SKINgenius is building an **AI-powered treatment simulation platform** that allows users to scan their face and visualize realistic "after treatment" results for cosmetic injectables (neuromodulators, dermal fillers) and energy-based devices. This creates a unique win-win opportunity with injectable manufacturers:

- **For SKINgenius:** Access to high-quality, standardized clinical before/after datasets to train our simulation models; product metadata for accurate recommendations; certified provider networks for referrals.
- **For Manufacturers:** Direct access to a pre-qualified, treatment-interested user base; product visibility in AI-driven recommendations; certified provider lead generation; exclusive market insights; co-marketing opportunities.

**Market Context:** The global medical aesthetics market is projected to grow from **$18.48B in 2024 to $55.99B by 2033** (CAGR ~13%). Injectable neuromodulators and dermal fillers represent the largest and fastest-growing segment. AI/AR treatment visualization is an emerging competitive differentiator — Galderma already launched **FACE by Galderma™** (powered by Crisalix AR) in 2023, and Allergan has **Allē Flash** (ML-powered personalized offers).

## 1. The Pitch — What SKINgenius Offers Manufacturers

### 1.1 Qualified, Treatment-Interested User Base

Unlike general social media or broad advertising, SKINgenius users are **actively evaluating treatments**:

| User Segment | Description | Value to Manufacturer |
|-------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Scan → Simulation Users** | Users who scan their face AND run treatment simulation | Highest intent — have visualized results on their own face |
| **Severity-Triggered Referrals** | Users with condition severity ≥7/10 flagged for professional care | Pre-qualified, urgent need, ready to book |
| **Goal-Setters** | Users who set skin goals (e.g., "reduce forehead lines") during onboarding | Self-declared treatment interest |
| **Repeat Scanners** | Users who re-scan monthly to track progress | Engaged, committed to improvement |
| **Age 35–55** | Core demographic (Gen X / older Millennials) | Highest disposable income, fastest-growing treatment segment |

**Projected Reach (Year 1):**
- **50,000+ monthly active users** (conservative estimate post-launch)
- **15,000+ treatment simulation runs/month** by Month 6
- **3,000+ severity-triggered referrals/month** to certified providers
- **70% female, 35–55 age bracket** — the core aesthetic treatment demographic

### 1.2 Product Visibility in AI Recommendations

When a user sees simulation results, SKINgenius recommends specific products by name:

```
User sees: "Forehead lines reduced by 60%"
AI recommends:
  → "Botox Cosmetic (onabotulinumtoxinA) — 20 units"
  → "Dysport (abobotulinumtoxinA) — 50 units"  
  → "Daxxify (daxibotulinumtoxinA-lanm) — 20 units"
     [Featured Partner — see certified providers]
```

**Placement Options:**
- **Standard placement:** Product name + expected units + duration
- **Featured placement:** Highlighted card with manufacturer branding + "certified provider" badge
- **Exclusive placement:** Single-product recommendation (Tier 3 partners only, for specific treatment types)

### 1.3 Certified Provider Lead Generation

SKINgenius maintains a **Certified Provider Network** — practitioners who have completed manufacturer training and are verified in our system.

**Lead Flow:**
1. User views simulation results → CTA: "Find a Certified Provider Near You"
2. SKINgenius shows **certified providers first** (sorted by distance, rating, availability)
3. User taps provider → views profile → books appointment
4. Manufacturer receives: lead data, booking conversion rate, treatment type demand

**Value:** Direct attribution from digital engagement → patient booking. Manufacturers currently rely on indirect metrics ("lift in prescriptions"); SKINgenius provides **end-to-end funnel visibility**.

### 1.4 Training Revenue from Certification Program

SKINgenius will launch a **Provider Certification Program** where practitioners complete training modules on:
- Treatment simulation interpretation
- Patient expectation management
- Product-specific injection techniques
- Safety protocols and contraindications

**Revenue Share:**
- Certification course fee: $299–$499 per practitioner
- SKINgenius retains 60% of fee
- **Manufacturer receives 40%** (for providing training content + certification authority)
- Manufacturer also gains: trained provider network, brand loyalty, competitive moat

SKINgenius aggregates **anonymized, opt-in data** on treatment demand by:

## 2. The Ask — What We Need From Manufacturers

### 2.1 Clinical Before/After Photo Datasets

**Purpose:** Train AI simulation models to generate realistic "after treatment" visualizations.

| Requirement | Specification |
|------------|---------------|
| **Quantity** | Minimum 5,000 matched before/after pairs per product |
| **Quality** | High-resolution (≥2MP), standardized lighting, neutral background |
| **Angles** | Frontal, 45° left, 45° right, optional profile |
| **Annotations** | Treatment type, product name, units/injections, injection points, patient age, skin type (Fitzpatrick I–VI) |
| **De-identification** | Face must be de-identified (eyes blacked, no identifying features) or consent obtained |
| **Consent** | IRB-approved or equivalent patient consent for AI training use |
| **Diversity** | Representation across Fitzpatrick skin types I–VI, ages 25–65, both sexes |
| **Format** | DICOM or high-res JPEG/PNG with JSON metadata |
| **Timeline** | Deliver in batches (1,000 every 2 weeks) starting Month 1 of partnership |

**Why this matters:** Current publicly available datasets (e.g., RealSelf, Aesthetic Surgery Journal) are limited, inconsistently labeled, and lack diversity. Manufacturer clinical trial data is the **gold standard** for training.

### 2.2 Product Metadata

| Data Field | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **INCI name** | International Nomenclature | "OnabotulinumtoxinA" |
| **Brand name(s)** | Marketed names by region | "Botox Cosmetic" (US), "Vistabel" (EU) |
| **Indications** | FDA/EMA-approved uses | "Glabellar lines, crow's feet, forehead lines" |
| **Mechanism of action** | How it works | "Blocks acetylcholine release at neuromuscular junction" |
| **Expected outcomes** | Clinical efficacy data | "Mean improvement 2.3 points on 4-point scale at Day 30" |
| **Duration** | Expected longevity | "3–4 months (glabellar), 4–6 months (crow's feet)" |
| **Dosing guidelines** | Standard units by treatment area | "Glabellar: 20 units (5 injection points)" |
| **Contraindications** | Who should not receive | "Pregnancy, neuromuscular disorders, allergy to albumin" |
| **Side effects** | Common and rare | "Bruising (15%), ptosis (2%), headache (5%)" |
| **Pricing** | Wholesale/average retail | "$12/unit wholesale, $15–$20/unit retail" |
| **Clinical trial citations** | PubMed IDs | "PMID: 29112345, PMID: 30267890" |
| **Competitor comparisons** | Head-to-head data | "Non-inferior to Botox at 20 units" |

**Format:** Structured JSON via API or quarterly CSV dump.

### 2.3 Certified Provider Network Access

| Requirement | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Provider directory** | List of trained/authorized practitioners with contact info |
| **Verification API** | Real-time check: "Is Dr. Smith still a certified [Product] injector?" |
| **Training status** | Completion dates, certification levels (basic/advanced/master) |
| **Geographic data** | Practice address, lat/lng for proximity search |
| **Practice details** | Photos, services offered, pricing, availability |
| **Update frequency** | Monthly refresh of provider status |

**Integration:** SKINgenius will build a connector to the manufacturer's provider database (or they can provide a read-only API).

### 2.4 Co-Marketing Support

| Asset | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Brand guidelines** | Logo usage, color palette, approved messaging |
| **Clinical photography** | High-res product images, before/afters for marketing |
| **Spokesperson access** | KOL availability for joint webinars/podcasts |
| **Press release coordination** | Joint announcements for partnership launches |
| **Event presence** | AAD/ASDS booth space for SKINgenius demo |
| **Digital assets** | Approved video content, animations, infographics |

### 2.5 Exclusivity (Optional — Tier 3 Only)

For Tier 3 partners, SKINgenius may offer:
- **Category exclusivity:** "For neuromodulators, Daxxify is the featured product in [Region]"
- **Time-limited exclusivity:** 90-day exclusive featured placement for new product launches
- **Data exclusivity:** Manufacturer insights not shared with competitors for 90 days

**Note:** SKINgenius will never recommend an unsafe or clinically inferior product solely because of partnership status. AI recommendations remain evidence-based.

### 3.2 Strategic Rationale for Prioritization

**Why Start with Revance and Evolus:**

1. **Lower barrier to entry:** Smaller companies have fewer approval layers. A VP-level decision can greenlight a pilot.
2. **Greater need:** New entrants need every advantage to compete with Botox. SKINgenius offers differentiated visibility.
3. **Digital-native:** Both companies have shown willingness to experiment (Evolus's social media campaigns, Revance's direct-to-consumer initiatives).
4. **Proof of concept:** Success with Tier A manufacturers creates case studies to pitch Allergan and Galderma.
5. **Faster iteration:** Pilot agreements can be signed in 30–60 days vs. 6–12 months for large pharma.

**Why Galderma Before Allergan:**
- Galderma already has AR infrastructure (FACE by Galderma) → understands the value of visualization
- Allergan's Allē program is mature → harder to integrate with, more competitive threat perceived
- Galderma's "consumer journey" strategy (see their 2023 investor day) aligns with SKINgenius

## 5. Revenue Model — How SKINgenius Makes Money

### 5.1 Revenue Streams

#### Stream 1: Referral Fees Per Booking

**Model:** When a SKINgenius user books an appointment with a certified provider through our platform, we earn a referral fee.

| Treatment Category | Average Treatment Value | Referral Fee Range | Market Benchmark |
|-------------------|------------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Neuromodulator (Botox/Dysport/etc.)** | $300–$600 | $30–$75 per booking | 10–15% of treatment value |
| **Dermal Filler (Juvederm/Restylane/etc.)** | $600–$1,200 | $60–$150 per booking | 10–15% of treatment value |
| **Biostimulator (Sculptra/Radiesse)** | $800–$2,000 | $80–$250 per booking | 10–15% of treatment value |
| **Energy-Based Device (Laser/RF/Ultrasound)** | $500–$3,000 | $50–$300 per booking | 10–15% of treatment value |
| **Combination Treatment** | $1,500–$5,000 | $150–$500 per booking | 10–15% of treatment value |

**Pricing Rationale:**
- Market rate for medical/aesthetic referrals is **10–15% of treatment value**
- Allē (Allergan loyalty program) offers $50 for friend referrals — our fee is competitive
- Med spa software (AestheticsPro, Zenoti) charges similar booking/referral fees
- Digital health platforms (ZocDoc, Healthgrades) charge $50–$200 per booked appointment

**SKINgenius Advantage:**
- Higher conversion rate than general directories (users have already visualized results)
- Pre-qualified users (severity scoring, goal-setting)
- End-to-end tracking (scan → simulation → provider → booking → outcome)

**Projected Revenue (Year 1):**
- 3,000 referrals/month × $75 average fee × 12 months = **$2.7M ARR**
- At 50% partner-certified providers = **$1.35M ARR from manufacturer referrals**

## 10. Risk Analysis & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Manufacturer refuses to share clinical data** (privacy, competitive concerns) | Medium | High | Start with publicly available data + synthetic data. Use Tier 1 as proof of concept. Offer stricter NDAs and data handling audits. |
| **AI simulation accuracy insufficient** | Medium | Critical | Continuous model improvement. Start with neuromodulators (predictable) before fillers (more variable). Clinical advisory board review. |
| **Regulatory scrutiny** (FDA, FTC claims) | Low-Medium | High | Clear disclaimers: "Simulation is illustrative, not guaranteed." Never claim FDA approval for simulation. Medical advisor sign-off on all claims. |
| **Manufacturer partnership takes too long** | High (for Allergan) | Medium | Prioritize Tier A/B companies first. Use pilot success as leverage. Keep pipeline full so no single deal is make-or-break. |
| **Competitive response** (Allergan builds consumer app) | Low | High | SKINgenius is product-agnostic (manufacturer-neutral) — our value is comparison + referral, not single-brand promotion. Incumbents want to promote their own products only. |
| **Provider network quality** (certified providers deliver poor outcomes) | Medium | High | Robust provider vetting, user reviews, outcome tracking. Remove providers with poor ratings. Manufacturer certification ≠ SKINgenius endorsement. |
| **Data breach** (clinical photos leaked) | Low | Critical | De-identification mandatory. HIPAA-compliant storage. Encryption at rest and in transit. Regular security audits. |
| **User trust** ("This is just advertising") | Medium | Medium | Clear labeling of featured partners. Always show alternative products. Algorithmic transparency reports. No pay-to-play for safety warnings. |
